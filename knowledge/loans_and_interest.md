# Loans and Deposit Interest Rates

> Demo Bank sample policy (fictional, for demonstration only).

## Personal loan
Personal loans are available from INR 50,000 to INR 25,00,000 for 12 to 60 months. The interest rate ranges from 10.5 percent to 16 percent per annum depending on the credit profile. A processing fee of 1.5 percent of the loan amount applies.

## Home loan eligibility
Salaried applicants aged 21 to 60 with a minimum monthly income of INR 30,000 and a credit score of 700 or more are eligible. Required documents are identity and address proof, the last 6 months bank statements and the last 3 salary slips.

## Fixed deposit interest rate
Fixed deposit interest rates range from 6.5 percent to 7.25 percent per annum for tenures from 1 to 5 years. Senior citizens receive an extra 0.50 percent.

## Loan enquiries by email
Loan applications are not processed by email. A relationship manager contacts the customer after the enquiry is registered through the website or branch.
