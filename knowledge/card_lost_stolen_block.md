# Lost, Stolen or Blocked Cards

> Demo Bank sample policy (fictional, for demonstration only).

## How to block a lost or stolen card
Block the card immediately through net banking, the mobile app or the 24x7 customer care line. A blocked card cannot be used for any new transaction. Do not wait for an email reply when a card is lost or stolen, because email is not monitored round the clock.

## Replacement card
A replacement card is issued after blocking. It is delivered to the registered address within 7 working days. A replacement fee of INR 200 plus GST applies, which is waived if the card was blocked because of fraud.

## Card PIN and OTP
The bank never asks for the card PIN, CVV, OTP or password by email, phone or SMS. Never share these details with anyone, including bank staff.

## Temporary block
A card can be temporarily blocked and unblocked from the mobile app, for example while travelling or when the card is misplaced.
