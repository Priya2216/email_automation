# Unauthorized Transactions and Disputes

> Demo Bank sample policy (fictional, for demonstration only).

## How to report an unauthorized transaction
Report the transaction as soon as you notice it through net banking, the mobile app or the customer care line, and block the card. Reporting by email is also accepted, but the card should be blocked first. Quote the card number ending, transaction date and amount.

## Reporting timeline and customer liability
If an unauthorized transaction is reported within 3 working days, the customer has zero liability. If it is reported between 4 and 7 working days, liability is limited to the transaction value or INR 10,000, whichever is lower. After 7 working days the case is reviewed individually.

## Dispute resolution time
A dispute is acknowledged within 24 hours. A provisional credit is given within 10 working days for unauthorized transactions, and the final resolution is completed within 90 days.

## Chargeback
For merchant disputes such as a duplicate charge or goods not received, the bank raises a chargeback with the card network after the customer submits the supporting documents.
