# Savings Account Charges

> Demo Bank sample policy (fictional, for demonstration only).

## Minimum average balance
The minimum average monthly balance is INR 10,000 for metro and urban branches and INR 5,000 for semi-urban and rural branches. If the balance falls below this, a shortfall charge of INR 150 plus GST is applied for the month.

## ATM transactions
Five free ATM transactions per month are allowed at the bank's own ATMs and three at other bank ATMs in metro cities. Each additional transaction costs INR 21 plus GST.

## Account maintenance
There is no account maintenance fee on a regular savings account. SMS alerts are free for the first 3 months and then cost INR 15 per quarter.

## Interest rate on savings account
Interest is paid at 3 percent per annum on the daily closing balance up to INR 1 crore, and is credited quarterly.
