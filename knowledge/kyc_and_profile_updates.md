# KYC and Profile Updates

> Demo Bank sample policy (fictional, for demonstration only).

## KYC documents required
For KYC, a valid proof of identity (Aadhaar, passport, voter ID or driving licence), proof of address and a recent photograph are required, along with the PAN card or Form 60.

## Updating address or mobile number
Changes to the registered address or mobile number cannot be made by email for security reasons. Update them through net banking with OTP verification, or visit the branch with the original documents.

## Periodic KYC update
KYC must be refreshed every 10 years for low-risk customers, every 8 years for medium-risk customers and every 2 years for high-risk customers.

## Nominee
A nominee can be added or changed through net banking or by submitting the nomination form at the branch.
