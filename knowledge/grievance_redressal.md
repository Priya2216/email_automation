# Complaints and Grievance Redressal

> Demo Bank sample policy (fictional, for demonstration only).

## How to raise a complaint
Complaints can be raised by email, the customer care line, the mobile app or at any branch. Every complaint receives a reference number and an acknowledgement within 24 hours.

## Resolution timeline
The bank aims to resolve complaints within 7 working days. Complex cases may take up to 30 days, and the customer is informed of the reason for the delay.

## Escalation levels
If the complaint is not resolved, the customer can escalate to the Nodal Officer, then to the Principal Nodal Officer. If there is still no satisfactory resolution within 30 days, the customer may approach the banking ombudsman.

## Priority handling
Emails reporting fraud, or from customers who are upset or have written more than once, are flagged as priority and a senior officer contacts the customer within 24 hours.
