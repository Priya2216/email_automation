# Cheque Book and Account Statements

> Demo Bank sample policy (fictional, for demonstration only).

## Cheque book request
A new cheque book can be requested through net banking, the mobile app or at the branch. A cheque book of 25 leaves is free once every quarter; additional cheque books cost INR 3 per leaf. Delivery takes 5 to 7 working days to the registered address. Cheque book requests are not processed by email.

## Account statement
Account statements for up to the last 12 months can be requested by email and are sent as a PDF to the registered email address. Older statements need a branch request and a fee of INR 100 per statement year.

## Statement for loan or visa purposes
A signed and stamped statement can be collected from the home branch. It is ready within 2 working days.
