# Digital Banking Security

> Demo Bank sample policy (fictional, for demonstration only).

## What the bank will never ask
The bank never asks for your PIN, password, CVV or OTP over email, phone or SMS. Do not click on links in emails that ask you to verify your account.

## Net banking password reset
The net banking password can be reset using the Forgot Password option with OTP and debit card details. Password resets are not done by email.

## Suspicious emails
Forward suspicious emails to the bank's security team and delete them. The bank sends replies only from its official support address.
