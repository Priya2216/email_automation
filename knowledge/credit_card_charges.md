# Credit Card Fees and Charges

> Demo Bank sample policy (fictional, for demonstration only).

## Annual fee
The annual fee is INR 999 plus GST for the Classic credit card and INR 2,499 plus GST for the Platinum card. The annual fee is waived for a year when card spends in the previous year are INR 1,50,000 or more.

## Late payment charges
If the minimum amount due is not paid by the due date, a late payment fee applies: INR 500 when the total amount due is up to INR 5,000, INR 750 when it is between INR 5,001 and INR 25,000, and INR 1,000 when it is above INR 25,000. Late payment may also attract interest on the outstanding amount.

## Interest rate on outstanding balance
Interest on unpaid card balances is charged at 3.5 percent per month (42 percent per annum). There is no interest on purchases when the full statement amount is paid by the due date.

## Cash advance charges
A cash advance fee of 2.5 percent of the amount withdrawn, with a minimum of INR 500, applies per withdrawal. Interest is charged from the date of withdrawal until the amount is repaid.

## Foreign currency (forex) mark-up
A forex mark-up of 3.5 percent applies on card transactions made in a foreign currency or processed outside India.

## Credit limit
Credit limit changes are not handled by email. Customers can request a limit review through net banking or the customer care phone line.
